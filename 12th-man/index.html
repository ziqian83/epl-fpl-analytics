How it loads
Read-only GET through one named public relay: r.jina.ai, Jina AI's Reader service (a documented, free, rate-limited product). The relay sees the team ID in the request URL, like any site you visit. No login, no keys, nothing is written back. If the relay is down or its free terms change, loading fails loudly - it never falls back to a second operator without telling you.

The next five gameweeks, four policies

Four different policies simulated over GW9-13 under the real rules - formation, club cap, budget, transfer carry, hits, chip state, captain each week. When two policies land on the same moves, the cards say so: convergence is shown, never hidden. Totals are cumulative weak estimates and stay uncalibrated: small gaps between cards are not meaningful. Five gameweeks is our chosen comparison window, not a data limit - the official fixture list runs the full season; cumulative uncalibrated estimates compound with length, so the window stays short. Prices are frozen and trailing form is held constant; both are stated, not hidden.

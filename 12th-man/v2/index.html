How it loads
Read-only GET through one named public relay: r.jina.ai, Jina AI's Reader service (a documented, free, rate-limited product). The relay sees the team ID in the request URL, like any site you visit. No login, no keys, nothing is written back. If the relay is down or its free terms change, loading fails loudly - it never falls back to a second operator without telling you.

What I would do

The feasible plays side by side, each in both lenses: this gameweek, and the next six. Expected points come from a small open model whose rolling holdouts ordered candidate swaps about half a percentage point ahead of a naive baseline - a weak signal. Edges under ~1 xP sit inside the model's decision margin and are labelled, never sold. Cards state what the model flags; the risk choice is yours, every week.
